# SIH Problem 26044: Verified Skill Ecosystem

## Problem Description
Current recruitment workflows suffer from extreme resume inflation and lack of empirical skill verification. Simultaneously, higher education institutions lack data-driven visibility into industry skill demands vs. student capabilities, resulting in persistent curriculum deficits.

## Key Requirements Solved:
1. **Multi-Modal Skill Verification:** Moving beyond unverified assertions to multi-tier proof.
2. **Verified Digital Skill Passport:** High-trust credential with cryptographic audit trails and QR verification.
3. **Institutional Demand vs. Availability Analytics:** Helping universities compare regional corporate demands with student cohorts to reform course syllabi.
4. **Faculty Development & Immersion:** Faculty internships, FDPs, and collaborative industry projects.
